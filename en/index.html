08 / Environmental challenges

The balance is fragile.

Threats and responses

What we need to prevent.

Solid waste and plasticsThey can reach rivers and oceans, harm animals and damage the landscape.
Habitat lossLoss of vegetation reduces areas where wildlife can feed, rest and shelter.
Disturbing wildlifeNoise, feeding wild animals and getting too close can change their behavior.
Tourism pressurePoorly managed tourism can increase erosion, waste and disturbances.

Preventing damage is more effective than trying to repair it later.

09 / Responsible tourism

Visit without leaving a harmful footprint.

A responsible experience combines enjoyment, safety and respect for the place and the wildlife that lives there.

  • Bring a reusable water bottle and take all your waste with you.
  • Keep your distance from wildlife; never touch or feed animals.
  • Stay on designated trails and respect signs and sensitive nesting areas.
  • Leave shells, plants and living organisms where you find them.
  • Always follow the guidance of tour guides and local authorities.
  • Support local businesses and community products.
